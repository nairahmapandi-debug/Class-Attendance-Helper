CLASS ATTENDANCE RECORD FIELDS

1. Student ID
A unique identification number assigned to each student to identify their records.

2. Student Name
The complete name of the student whose attendance is being recorded.

3. Date
The specific date when the student attendance is recorded.

4. Attendance Status
Indicates whether the student is Present, Absent, or Late.

5. Time In
Records the time when the student arrives in class.

6. Remarks
Provides additional notes about a student's attendance, such as the reason for being late or absent.
